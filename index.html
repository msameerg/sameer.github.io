Featured Case Study: Credit Risk Intelligence

The Mission

Building a robust, real-time risk assessment engine that moves beyond simple credit scores to analyze a borrower's full financial health, distinguishing "safe" borrowers from high-risk defaults.

The Engineering Challenge: Defeating Data Leakage

The biggest hurdle wasn't the algorithm—it was the data. I identified "leaky" variables (interest rates/charges) that only exist post-approval. By performing a rigorous feature audit, I stripped the model to pre-approval variables only, ensuring real-world reliability over "fake" 100% accuracy.
